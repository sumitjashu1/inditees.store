QUESTIONS?

Want to know something else? Just message us.

Do you have a physical shop? +

INDITEES currently operates as an online-first business. You can request product photos and discuss your order with us on WhatsApp before confirming.

Can I order a custom design? +

Yes. Share your design idea, preferred T-shirt, size, colour and quantity. We'll discuss printing options and provide a quote.

Can I place a bulk order? +

Yes, bulk enquiries are welcome. Send us your required quantity, sizes and design requirements for a quotation.

How do payment and delivery work? +

Payment method, shipping charges, expected dispatch date and any return or exchange terms are confirmed directly before you pay.
